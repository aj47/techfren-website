Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

TechFren Uploader

TechFren Uploader is a private tool used only by AJ (techfren) to upload and manage videos on his own YouTube channel, Tech Friend AJ. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's account or data.

What data it uses

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and to read and update the titles, descriptions, thumbnails and settings of videos on the Tech Friend AJ channel. It does not request, collect or process data about viewers or other YouTube users.

How data is stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on AJ's own computer. They are not sent to or stored on any server operated by techfren.

Sharing

No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, shared with or transferred to any third party. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
